Secure It

Your passwords. Your Drive. Only yours.

Secure It is a zero-knowledge password manager for Android, iOS, Windows and your browser. Your vault is encrypted on your device, and you can sync it through your own Google Drive. We run no servers, so nobody else holds your passwords or your key.

What it does

  • Stores logins in a vault encrypted with AES-256-GCM; your master password is protected with Argon2id.
  • Unlocks with your fingerprint, generates strong passwords, and organises logins into categories.
  • Imports saved passwords from your browser (CSV) in one step.
  • Shares a login through a secure link that expires automatically.
  • 12-word recovery phrase and an Emergency Kit PDF so you never get locked out.

How Secure It uses your Google account

  • Linking Google Drive is optional and off by default.
  • The app requests only drive.file, so it can see only the files it creates itself (your encrypted vault.enc and any share files) and never the rest of your Drive, plus userinfo.email to show which account is linked.
  • Everything is encrypted on your device before upload. Neither Google nor we can read it.
  • Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or read by any person. Secure It's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
